Building an ISMS

Practical work on the implementation of an information security management system, from organisational context and scope to risk management and gap analysis.

  • ISO/IEC 27001
  • ISMS
  • Context & scope
  • Gap analysis

Purpose

Define what the ISMS covers, understand the organisation's context and connect risk assessment with a structured review of security requirements.

Work covered

  • Analyse the organisational context and the factors relevant to the ISMS.
  • Define the scope and boundaries of the management system.
  • Work through information security risk management as part of implementation.
  • Review the current position against requirements through gap analysis.

Skills applied

Scope definition, structured documentation, risk analysis and requirement review.
