My approach

Information sustains the business.
Managing its security helps protect it.

Organisations depend on information to operate, make decisions and maintain trust. Managing its security means understanding what needs protection, assessing risks and applying proportionate, verifiable controls that can be improved over time.

Confidentiality, integrity and availability give that work a practical purpose: protect information from unauthorised disclosure, preserve its reliability and keep it available when needed.

Understand the business

Processes, information, stakeholders and dependencies define what matters and where the ISMS applies.

Make risk-based decisions

Consider impact and likelihood to prioritise treatment and justify the controls selected.

Check and improve

Use evidence, internal review and corrective actions to assess how controls work in practice.
