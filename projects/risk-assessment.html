AENOR coursework

Risk assessment & treatment

  • Assets
  • Impact
  • Treatment
  • Residual risk

A fictitious hospital case connecting asset dependencies, threat assessment and treatment decisions.

Training context

Five connected exercises consolidated into a risk report for Hospital Monte Aneto, a fictitious organisation used in the training. The assessment works with the information and assumptions of that scenario.

Work carried out

  • Value assets and the impact of information security events using the dimensions considered in the case.
  • Analyse asset dependencies and the propagation of impact to supporting resources.
  • Assess threats and likelihood, evaluate risk scenarios and document treatment proposals.
  • Review the residual-risk levels resulting from the proposed treatment within the exercise.

Assessment rationale

An asset's relevance depends on the information and processes it supports. Linking dependencies, impact and treatment makes the assessment easier to explain and helps distinguish a proposed control from evidence of an effective control.

Documented output

A consolidated CP01–CP05 report bringing together asset valuation, dependencies, threat analysis, risk assessment and treatment.

Scope & limitations

Simulated case. Residual-risk values are estimates within the exercise, not measured business results or evidence that controls were deployed in a real hospital.

Supporting work

Original materials remain private. We can discuss the methodology and which self-authored extracts can be shared after review.
