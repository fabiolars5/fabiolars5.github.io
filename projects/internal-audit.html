AENOR coursework

Internal audit preparation

  • ISO/IEC 27001
  • Evidence
  • Sampling
  • Audit planning

Four practical assignments covering scope, documentation, control verification and a simulated audit plan.

Training context

A series of internal-audit exercises based on fictitious organisations. The work progresses from evaluating the scope and documentation to considering evidence, sampling and the organisation of an audit.

Work carried out

  • Evaluate the scope, relevant functions, organisational boundaries and third-party interfaces.
  • Review management-system documentation and the corrective action plan in the scenario.
  • Identify audit techniques and evidence for responsibilities, suppliers, physical access and network controls.
  • Prepare a simulated four-day audit plan with activities, roles, timings, meetings and sampled areas.

Assessment rationale

An audit conclusion needs evidence related to a defined criterion and scope. Sampling and interviews should be planned to test what happens in practice, while keeping the auditor's evaluation separate from a consultant's implementation recommendations.

Documented output

Four completed assignments: scope review, documentary and corrective-action review, control audit techniques and an audit plan.

Scope & limitations

Simulated audit preparation. It does not represent a completed certification audit or independent audit engagement for a real client.

Supporting work

Original materials remain private. We can discuss the methodology and which self-authored extracts can be shared after review.
