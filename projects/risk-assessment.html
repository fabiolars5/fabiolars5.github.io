Information security risk

Application of the information security risk cycle: asset and threat identification, scenario assessment, treatment planning and residual-risk follow-up.

  • ISO/IEC 27005
  • Risk assessment
  • Treatment
  • Residual risk

Purpose

Turn identified security risks into prioritised, documented decisions, with a traceable link between assessment, treatment and ongoing review.

Work covered

  • Identify information assets, threats and vulnerabilities.
  • Assess and prioritise risk scenarios.
  • Document a risk treatment plan.
  • Review residual risk and follow-up actions for continuous improvement.

Skills applied

Asset analysis, risk prioritisation, treatment planning and traceability.
