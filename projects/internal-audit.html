Internal ISMS audit

Preparation and documentation of an internal ISMS audit, covering scope, documentation, evidence, corrective actions and assessment planning.

  • ISO/IEC 27001
  • ISO/IEC 27002
  • Evidence
  • Internal audit

Purpose

Develop a structured approach to audit preparation and evidence review, with attention to scope, findings, corrective actions and relevant security controls.

Work covered

  • Evaluate the audit scope and the information supporting it.
  • Review documentation and the corrective action plan.
  • Examine how ISO/IEC 27002 controls can be audited.
  • Plan an audit covering ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 27002.

Skills applied

Audit planning, documentation review and corrective-action analysis.
