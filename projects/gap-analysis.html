Case studies / Control review

AENOR coursework

Gap analysis

  • ISO/IEC 27002
  • Policies
  • Backups
  • Responsibilities

Review of policy governance, backup practices and disciplinary arrangements against the control expectations in a training scenario.

Training context

The assignment describes three situations with incomplete practices or documentation. I examined the information available, identified relevant interviewees and documented gaps and improvement recommendations.

Work carried out

  • Review the distinction between an IT good-practice manual and an approved, communicated and reviewed information security policy framework.
  • Examine backup responsibilities, media inventory, configuration knowledge and evidence of restoration tests.
  • Consider the need for defined, communicated responsibilities and procedures when addressing security-related misconduct.

Assessment rationale

The existence of a document or an automated task does not, by itself, establish that a control is working. The review considers approval, responsibilities, communication, execution and evidence before drawing a conclusion.

Documented output

A control-by-control analysis identifying interviewees, gaps and reasoned recommendations for the three scenarios.

Scope & limitations

Training analysis, not a full organisational compliance assessment or legal opinion. Recommendations were proposed within the case; implementation and effectiveness were not verified.
